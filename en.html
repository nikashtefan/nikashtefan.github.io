questions

how do the three formats differ?

«One task» is for when you want to start small and see how it works. «Package from the review» is 3 automations from your review, the most common choice. «AI assistant» is for when you need to cover a whole process, not a single step: from a request to the answer to the customer.

what if we need it long-term?

There's a subscription, price on request: I maintain what's already running and build 1–2 new tasks every month.

what if we also need to train the team?

That's a separate format: a workshop on your own tasks and a sprint with champions inside the team. Details are on a separate page (in Russian).

can you do research and prototypes too?

Yes. Analyzing interviews and reviews takes hours instead of weeks, and an idea gets tested with a prototype in a day. That's my core expertise from T-Bank, this page just shows what people ask for most.

what kind of team is a good fit?

5 to 15 people and one person who owns the decision. If you need to cover several departments with CRM and ERP integrations, I'll say it straight: that's a job for an implementation team, not for me alone.

how much of your time does it take?

For the free step, one 1–2 hour call. After that almost nothing: I build it myself, and from you I need access and half an hour to accept the work.

how do we measure the result?

Before the start and a month later: hours spent on the process and what that costs, response time to customers or share of lost requests.
