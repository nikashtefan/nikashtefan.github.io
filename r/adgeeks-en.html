Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

One cross-platform report with insights

  1. Numbers from DV360, Amazon DSP, Adform and Meta or Google Ads land in one table
  2. AI compares them with last period and the client's KPIs and finds what moved and why
  3. AI drafts the insights and next steps; the account manager checks and edits
  4. The client gets one report with clear conclusions instead of several platform exports
Takes off: hours spent pulling numbers from each platform and writing insights for every client
idea 2

Daily pacing and budget leak check

  1. Every day AI looks at spend pacing, frequency, viewability and conversions across all DSPs
  2. It flags leaks: overspend, high frequency, campaigns optimizing for clicks where reach is the goal
  3. The specialist gets a short list in Telegram or Slack: where money is lost and what to fix
  4. The specialist decides; findings go into the client report
Takes off: manual checks of every platform and the risk of spotting a problem at month end
idea 3

Answering client questions about results

  1. A client asks: "why did CPM go up in October?"
  2. AI looks at platform data and the report and drafts an answer with numbers
  3. The account manager checks and sends it
  4. Recurring questions get answered in the next report
Takes off: digging through platforms for numbers for every client question

Where to start: Start with one cross-platform report for 2-3 clients for 2 weeks. From you: 2-3 past reports clients liked, exports from the platforms for a couple of months and your report template.

What AI won't fix. AI does not know your agreements with clients or what was said on a call. Strategy and budget decisions stay with the team. AI only prepares drafts and flags deviations.
