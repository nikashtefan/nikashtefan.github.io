Academy of Digital Industries: where AI could take manual work off

Academy of Digital Industries team

80 mentors and constant hiring: an AI assistant can handle mentor contract paperwork
I looked at the academy website and the open roles on Recruitee. You have 80 mentors, and right now there are 9 open mentor roles in Tbilisi and Prague. The application form asks for a rate per workshop. It looks like for every new mentor someone collects the details and prepares the contract by hand, and this work grows as you grow.

What I looked at

  • On the website: 80 active mentors, 30 000+ graduates, 2 000+ courses delivered, 1 800+ partner companies · learn.academyofdigitalindustries.com, 10.11.2026
  • The careers page lists 9 open mentor roles: 4 in Tbilisi (Cloud Computing, Content Creator, Digital Marketing, accounting) and 5 in Prague · academyofdigitalindustries.recruitee.com, 10.11.2026
  • The Digital Marketing Mentor application asks for the expected fee in GEL for one workshop · academyofdigitalindustries.recruitee.com, 10.11.2026
  • The website describes mentors as industry practitioners selected for their experience and ability to teach · learn.academyofdigitalindustries.com, 10.11.2026

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Mentor contract without collecting details by hand

  1. A new mentor sends photos of documents and bank details via Telegram or a link
  2. AI cross-checks the details across documents, finds typos and empty fields, and asks the mentor to fix them right away
  3. AI fills in the contract from your template: rate per workshop, course, dates
  4. A manager gets a ready contract to check and sign
Takes off: back-and-forth with mentors for passport and bank details, filling in the contract template by hand
idea 2

Tracking delivered workshops for mentor payouts

  1. Course schedules and workshop check-offs go into one table
  2. AI counts how many workshops each mentor delivered this month
  3. AI prepares an act or payout summary and sends it to the mentor to confirm
  4. Accounting gets a list already agreed with the mentors
Takes off: checking hours and workshops with each mentor by hand before payout
idea 3

First screening of mentor applications

  1. Applications from Recruitee go to AI together with the form answers
  2. AI checks experience, required tools, Georgian, and readiness to work in Tbilisi
  3. AI makes a short candidate card and marks who is worth inviting to an interview
  4. The team only reviews the shortlist
Takes off: reading every CV for 9 open roles

Where to start: Start with contracts for new mentors in Tbilisi for 2 weeks. From you: the contract template, the list of details you collect from a mentor, and 3–5 anonymized examples of filled-in contracts.

What AI won't fix. AI will not decide whether a mentor is a good personal fit or how they will run a group. A person still agrees contract terms and handles disputed cases.
