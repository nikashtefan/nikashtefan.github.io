Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Monthly client report with conclusions

  1. Positions, traffic and conversions for the month come from Search Console, Analytics and your rank tracker
  2. AI compares them with last month and the plan, and finds what moved and why
  3. It writes the report in your template, in Georgian or English: what happened, what we did, what's next
  4. The account manager checks it and sends it the same day
Takes off: writing the monthly report and comments on the numbers for each client by hand
idea 2

Free SEO audit as a draft

  1. A lead asks for a free audit on the site
  2. AI runs the technical check, compares with 2–3 competitors and drafts the audit
  3. A specialist reviews and adds the strategy part
  4. The lead gets the audit faster, while interest is still high
Takes off: hours of manual work on audits for leads who may not convert
idea 3

Content briefs from the semantic core

  1. The keyword research for a client is ready
  2. AI groups queries by intent and drafts a brief for each page
  3. The SEO specialist approves the briefs
  4. Copywriters start with a clear brief instead of a spreadsheet
Takes off: manual work turning the semantic core into briefs for writers

Where to start: Take 5 clients for 2 weeks. I need 10 past monthly reports you are happy with and access to the data behind them.

What AI won't fix. AI won't build links or decide the SEO strategy for a client. It removes the routine of reporting and drafts, the expertise stays with your team.
