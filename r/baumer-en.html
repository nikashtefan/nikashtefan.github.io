Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Draft MEP estimate and spec from a request

  1. A manager dictates the project or uploads drawings and the brief: area, floors, HVAC, electrical, water supply
  2. AI builds a spec based on your past projects and price lists
  3. It prepares a draft BOQ and quote in your template
  4. A QS specialist checks and edits it
Takes off: building BOQs and specs by hand for every project
idea 2

Comparing supplier quotes

  1. 3–5 supplier quotes come in, in different formats
  2. AI puts them into one table, line by line
  3. It shows the differences in price, lead time and scope
  4. Procurement makes the decision
Takes off: merging supplier quotes by hand
idea 3

Knowledge base for the Academy and new engineers

  1. Internal rules, standards and codes go into one base
  2. A new engineer asks the assistant
  3. AI answers with a link to the document
  4. Senior engineers get interrupted less
Takes off: answering the same questions from new hires

Where to start: Start with one system type for 2 weeks, for example HVAC for offices. From you: 10–15 past BOQs, supplier price lists and your quote template.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace a QS specialist or an engineer on a complex project. It prepares a draft. People stay responsible for the numbers.
