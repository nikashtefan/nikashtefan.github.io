Beka's School: where AI could take manual work off

Natali Kenkadze, Co-Founder and General Manager

AI is already in your lessons: the next step is to give it level checks, scheduling and corporate requests
I read about Beka's School in OC Media and Georgia Today. You already give students an AI app and teach English to more than 80 companies. Around the lessons there is a lot of manual work: answering new students and HR managers, checking levels and building schedules.

What I looked at

  • About 40 staff including 25 native speakers, 12,000+ graduates; students use the Talkpal AI app · OC Media, 13.11.2026
  • 400-450 regular students (2023) · Georgia Today, 13.11.2026
  • 80+ corporate clients including Bank of Georgia, TBC and UNDP · Georgia Today, 13.11.2026

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

First answer and level check

  1. A new student or an HR manager writes
  2. AI answers about courses, formats and schedule
  3. AI runs a short first level check
  4. The team gets a ready student with a level and a booked trial lesson
Takes off: manual first answers and level tests
idea 2

Corporate requests

  1. HR sends a group: names, levels, goals
  2. AI suggests a program and a schedule
  3. It drafts the offer
  4. The manager checks and sends it
Takes off: hours spent on every corporate proposal
idea 3

Progress reports for companies

  1. AI collects attendance and test results by group
  2. It drafts a short report for HR
  3. The teacher adds comments
  4. The company gets a clear report every month
Takes off: manual reports to corporate clients

Where to start: Start with first answers and level checks for 2 weeks. From you: course descriptions, your level test and the most frequent questions.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace your teachers or the speaking practice. It only takes the admin work around lessons.
