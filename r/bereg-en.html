Chisty bereg: where AI could take manual work off

Viktor Podgaisky, director and co-founder

Offers from equipment questionnaires could take minutes instead of 1–2 business days
I looked at bereg.by: the about page, the equipment selection service and the list of objects. Chisty bereg has 330 people and more than 28,000 items, and after a client sends a questionnaire the offer comes within 1–2 business days. This looks like the place where AI could save engineers a lot of time.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Questionnaire to draft offer in minutes

  1. A filled questionnaire comes in by email: a pump unit, a heat substation or a heat meter
  2. AI reads the parameters, finds matching models in the catalog of 28,000 items and checks stock
  3. The engineer gets a draft offer with 2–3 options, specs and certificates attached
  4. The client gets the offer the same day, the engineer only checks and signs
Takes off: reading questionnaires and searching the catalog by hand for each request
idea 2

Designer's specification to priced basket

  1. A specification from a project comes in as PDF or Excel
  2. AI matches each line to catalog items and suggests analogs if the exact model is missing
  3. The manager gets a ready basket with prices and a list of lines to clarify
  4. Quotes for residential and commercial objects go out faster
Takes off: line by line matching of long specifications, which looks like a big part of work with designers
idea 3

Quick answers to technical questions

  1. A client asks about a product, a certificate or a replacement in email or chat
  2. AI finds the product card, the certificate and the catalog page
  3. The manager gets a ready answer with attachments
  4. Answers come in minutes and the technical team gets fewer simple calls
Takes off: searching certificates and catalogs for routine questions

Where to start: One week on one questionnaire type, for example circulation pumps. We need 20–30 past questionnaires with the offers that were sent and a catalog export. AI drafts offers for them and engineers compare with what they sent.

What AI won't fix. AI won't take responsibility for a hydraulic calculation of a complex object, an engineer still checks it. If pump data in the catalog is incomplete, it needs to be filled first.
