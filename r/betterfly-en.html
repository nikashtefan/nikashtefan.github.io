Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Donor progress report draft in the funder's format

  1. Campaign numbers, event lists, reach data and media mentions for the period are collected in one folder
  2. AI fills the funder's template (EU, UNDP, USAID) and writes the narrative sections
  3. The project manager checks facts and tone and edits
  4. The report goes to the client on time with less late-night work
Takes off: hours spent rewriting the same results into each donor's format
idea 2

Weekly media monitoring digest

  1. AI collects mentions of the project and client in Georgian and English media and social networks
  2. It groups them by topic and tone and pulls out key quotes
  3. The team gets a short digest every week
  4. The digest goes straight into the monthly or quarterly report
Takes off: manual searching and copying of media mentions
idea 3

Visibility compliance check before publishing

  1. A layout, post or video description is uploaded with the donor's visibility guidelines
  2. AI checks logos, disclaimers and required wording against the rules
  3. The designer gets a list of what to fix
  4. Materials pass the client's review on the first try more often
Takes off: first manual review of every material against EU or USAID rules

Where to start: Start with one donor report for one current project for 2 weeks. From you: the funder's template, 1-2 past reports for this project and the raw data for the period.

What AI won't fix. AI does not know the context of your relationship with the donor or the project's internal decisions. The project manager checks every fact and owns the final report. AI only prepares the draft.
