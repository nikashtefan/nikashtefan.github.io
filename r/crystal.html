MicroBank Crystal: where AI could take manual work off

Ilia Revia, CEO

AI scoring gives the committee a number. The loan application and credit memo are still written by hand
I read your 2025 Integrated Report and recent press releases. EVA already runs 4 routine processes and the board approved AI scoring, so the bank is past the question of whether to use AI. The next step I see is the loan officer: they still fill in the application and prepare the case for the credit committee themselves, while cost-to-income is at 62% and operational efficiency is one of your stated priorities.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Loan officer assistant: application draft and credit memo

  1. The officer uploads photos from the client visit, a voice note and bank statements
  2. AI fills in a draft application and writes the memo for the credit committee
  3. The officer sees the draft with flagged mismatches in the data and fixes what is wrong
  4. The case reaches the committee faster and in the same format every time
Takes off: manual typing of the application and writing the committee memo for each loan
idea 2

Early warning after the loan is issued

  1. Payment history and short check-ins with the client about their business
  2. AI spots signs that a borrower may fall behind
  3. The branch gets a short list of clients to call this week
  4. Problems are caught earlier than at the quarterly review
Takes off: part of the manual quarterly monitoring done by branch heads and team leads
idea 3

Reports for lenders: green and women-led loan tagging

  1. Loan descriptions and portfolio data
  2. AI tags loans as green or women-led and drafts covenant and impact reports
  3. The finance team checks the draft instead of building it from scratch
  4. Reports for FMO, ADB and other lenders take less time
Takes off: manual tagging and assembling reports in each lender's format

Where to start: One pilot for 2 weeks: the credit memo for one loan type in one branch. From you: 10 to 20 anonymized past applications with their memos and the committee template.

What AI won't fix. AI will not make the credit decision and will not replace the scoring model. It prepares the paperwork, the officer and the committee still decide.
