Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Subcontractor contract the moment documents arrive

  1. A subcontractor sends company details, scope and the agreed price
  2. AI fills your contract template, specification and payment schedule
  3. Procurement or legal checks it
  4. The contract goes out for signature the same day
Takes off: days of waiting for a contract and retyping details into templates
idea 2

Quote comparison for procurement

  1. Suppliers send quotes in PDF and Excel
  2. AI pulls items, prices, lead times and terms into one table
  3. The manager sees gaps and the best offer
  4. The choice stays with the manager
Takes off: manual retyping of quotes into Excel for every request
idea 3

Draft acts and monthly reports per site

  1. Site engineers send work logs and photos
  2. AI drafts acts of completed work and a progress summary
  3. The engineer checks and signs
  4. The client gets the report on time
Takes off: copying the same numbers into dozens of acts every month

Where to start: Start with subcontractor contracts on one site for 2 weeks. I would need your contract template and 10–20 past contracts to check AI against your real documents.

What AI won't fix. AI will not negotiate with subcontractors or check quality on site. It prepares drafts, people decide.
