ELT Group: where AI could take manual work off

Mamuka Makharadze, CEO

Buyers from 61 countries write in different languages: AI can answer each of them at any hour
I looked at the ELT Group website and an interview about the company. You build premium residential and hotel projects in Batumi and sell to buyers from dozens of countries. On the site a buyer can leave a callback request or write in WhatsApp, so it looks like each request waits for a manager who speaks the right language.

What I looked at

  • 11 residential complexes, 350,000 m² built, about $150M invested · Gulf News, 13.11.2026
  • Buyers from 61 countries; partners include Barceló and Wyndham · Gulf News, 13.11.2026
  • On the market since 2017 · GRI Institute, 13.11.2026
  • Website in Georgian, English and Russian with a callback form and a WhatsApp link, no chat · eltgroup.ge, 13.11.2026

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Answering buyers 24/7 in their language

  1. A buyer writes in WhatsApp or on the site at night, in any language
  2. AI answers about projects, layouts, payment plans and the hotel program
  3. AI asks about budget, timing and purpose: living, rental or investment
  4. In the morning the manager gets a ready lead with all answers
Takes off: requests that wait until morning or for a manager who speaks the language
idea 2

Matching units to the budget

  1. The buyer names a budget and what matters: sea view, size, rental income
  2. AI looks through available units across your 11 complexes
  3. AI sends 2-3 options with plans and prices from your price list
  4. The manager continues with a buyer who already picked a favorite
Takes off: manual selection of units for every request
idea 3

Follow-up after the first contact

  1. AI remembers who asked what and when
  2. After a few days it reminds the buyer and offers a call or a visit
  3. Answers go back into the lead card
  4. The manager sees who is warm and who went quiet
Takes off: leads lost after the first message

Where to start: Start with WhatsApp answers for one complex for 2 weeks. From you: the price list, layouts, FAQ that managers answer most often, and 20-30 past chats as examples.

What AI won't fix. AI will not negotiate the price or replace a site visit. Contracts and final terms stay with your sales team, AI only prepares the buyer.
