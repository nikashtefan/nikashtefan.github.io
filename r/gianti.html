Gianti Logistics: where AI could take manual work off

George Makhatadze, General Manager

Every cargo request is read and quoted by hand. AI can prepare the first draft of the quote
I read about the wind turbine project in Breakbulk and Project Logistics Alliance and looked through your website. Gianti handles very complex cargo, but requests still come in through a contact form and email in any format. It looks like a lot of manager time goes into reading a request and asking for missing details before the real work starts.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Draft quote from an incoming request

  1. A client or partner sends a cargo request by email or the contact form
  2. AI pulls out cargo type, dimensions, weight, route and dates
  3. AI asks the client for missing details and prepares a draft quote
  4. The manager checks it and sends it the same day
Takes off: reading every request by hand and chasing missing details by email
idea 2

Document checks before customs

  1. Invoices, packing lists, bills of lading and CMRs arrive for a shipment
  2. AI compares them with each other and with the declaration
  3. The broker sees a short list of mismatches
  4. Errors are fixed before filing, not after a delay at the border
Takes off: line-by-line manual comparison of shipping documents
idea 3

Daily project reports

  1. Supervisors and drivers send notes, photos and voice messages from the route
  2. AI turns them into one daily report for the client and HSE
  3. The project manager reviews and sends it
  4. The client gets a clear report every day without extra evenings for the team
Takes off: manual report writing on large projects like the wind turbine move

Where to start: Start with draft quotes: in 2 weeks build a prototype on 20 to 30 past requests and compare AI drafts with the quotes your managers sent. We would need sample requests with final quotes and your basic rate logic.

What AI won't fix. AI will not plan a route through the mountains or replace engineers on complex project cargo. It takes over the routine reading and drafting so managers have more time for that work.
