The Grandeur Hotel: where AI could take manual work off

Natia Gogidze, CEO and General Manager

140 rooms and guests from abroad: AI can answer their WhatsApp questions 24/7 and pass ready bookings to reception
I looked at the hotel website, the Georgia travel registry and reviews on Trip.com. Guests come through Booking, Trip.com and your own booking page, and for questions the site offers a WhatsApp link. It looks like someone at reception answers every message by hand, in different languages and at any hour.

What I looked at

  • 140 rooms in Batumi, Lech and Maria Kaczyński St. 5 · georgia.travel, 13.11.2026
  • Restaurant Delmare, spa and pool; own booking page with guest account · grandeurhotel.ge, 13.11.2026
  • From about $150 per night, guest rating 8.5/10 · Trip.com, 13.11.2026
  • Website in English and Georgian, WhatsApp link, no chat · grandeurhotel.ge, 13.11.2026

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Answering guests in WhatsApp 24/7

  1. A guest writes at night: rooms, prices, transfer, spa hours
  2. AI answers in the guest's language using your rules and prices
  3. If the guest wants to book, AI collects dates, room type and contacts
  4. Reception gets a ready booking request and only confirms it
Takes off: night and peak-hour messages that wait for reception
idea 2

Before arrival

  1. Two days before arrival AI writes to the guest
  2. It offers a transfer, spa or a table at Delmare
  3. AI collects arrival time and wishes
  4. Reception sees everything in one list
Takes off: manual pre-arrival calls and lost extra sales
idea 3

Replies to reviews

  1. A new review appears on Booking or Google
  2. AI drafts a reply in the guest's language
  3. The manager checks and publishes
  4. Recurring complaints are collected in a weekly summary
Takes off: time spent on reviews and missed patterns in complaints

Where to start: Start with WhatsApp answers for 2 weeks. From you: room types and prices, transfer and spa rules, the 20 most frequent guest questions and a few past chats.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace the reception team on check-in or handle complaints on the spot. Prices, discounts and exceptions stay with your managers.
