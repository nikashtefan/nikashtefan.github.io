Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Material list and quote from drawings

  1. A roof plan, facade drawing or just area and type comes in from the client
  2. AI calculates the list by GRC catalogue and system rules: sheets, membrane, insulation, gutters, fasteners
  3. It builds the quote in your template and flags what to double-check
  4. A manager checks and sends it the same day
Takes off: manual takeoff and quote building for every project
idea 2

Helper for installers and partners

  1. An installer asks which system fits a roof or facade
  2. AI answers from GRC system specs: which layers, which components, in what order
  3. Hard questions go to your technical team with a short summary
  4. Partners get answers without waiting for a call back
Takes off: repeated consultation calls on the same questions
idea 3

Developer tender BOQ matching

  1. A developer sends a BOQ for a tender
  2. AI matches each line to GRC products and marks what you don't carry
  3. It drafts the offer in your format
  4. Sales checks and submits
Takes off: line-by-line manual matching of tender BOQs

Where to start: Take one system, for example pitched roof or facade insulation, for 2 weeks. We need 20–30 past requests with the quotes that went out, and the product catalogue.

What AI won't fix. AI won't measure a roof on site or decide on a non-standard detail. Final quantities are approved by your engineer or manager.
