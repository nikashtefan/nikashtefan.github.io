INN Group: where AI could take manual work off

Erekle Kokaia, Executive Director

Guests from many countries write in their own languages. AI can answer them 24/7 and pass bookings on

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Guest assistant in every language, 24/7

  1. A guest writes at night in Hebrew, Chinese or German about dates, prices or a transfer
  2. AI answers in the guest's language using your rooms, rates and hotel rules
  3. When the guest is ready, AI collects the booking details
  4. A manager confirms a ready booking in the morning instead of starting the conversation from scratch
Takes off: answering repeated questions by hand and losing guests who wrote outside working hours
idea 2

Onboarding helper for new front desk staff

  1. A new employee has a question about hotel standards or procedures
  2. They ask the assistant in chat
  3. The assistant answers from your internal rules and examples
  4. New staff get up to speed faster and senior staff are interrupted less
Takes off: part of the in-house training that falls on experienced staff
idea 3

Review replies in four hotels

  1. A guest leaves a review on Booking or Google in any language
  2. AI drafts a reply in that language in your tone
  3. A manager approves it in one click
  4. Every review gets a reply without extra hours for the team
Takes off: writing review replies by hand across several hotels and languages

Where to start: Start with the guest assistant for one hotel, for example Gudauri Inn: in 2 weeks build a prototype on 50 to 100 past guest conversations. We would need example chats, room types and rates, and the hotel rules.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace warm service at the hotel or decide on special cases like discounts and complaints. Those still go to a person, AI just takes the routine questions off the team.
