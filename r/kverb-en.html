KVERB: where AI could take manual work off

Tetiana Brevko, Co-Owner & CEO

KVERB sells machines from 25 brands through request forms: AI can draft the quote while the manager talks
I looked at kverb.eu, the About page and the news from this autumn. Every product card leads to a Leave a request button and there are no prices, so it looks like each request needs a person to pick the machine and send an offer. With 20,000+ clients and 25 manufacturers in the catalog, that is a lot of offers to build by hand.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Quote draft from a request in a couple of minutes

  1. A request comes from the site form, phone notes or email: what the client wants to make, material, sizes
  2. AI matches it with the KVERB catalog and brand specs, picks 1–3 machines and options, notes what is in stock and what is to order
  3. The manager gets a ready quote draft with specs and questions to clarify
  4. The manager checks, adds the price and sends the offer the same day
Takes off: looks like today a manager searches specs across 25 brand catalogs and builds each offer by hand
idea 2

Spec answers for clients and dealers

  1. A question comes: will this lathe handle this part, what is the difference between two models
  2. AI looks into manufacturer manuals and the site catalog and finds the answer with the source
  3. The manager or dealer gets a short answer with the right page from the manual
  4. Clients get an answer faster, engineers are asked less often
Takes off: repeated technical questions that probably go to engineers now
idea 3

Service request intake

  1. A client describes a problem with a machine in a message or call
  2. AI collects model, serial number, symptoms and photos and checks past service records
  3. The service team gets a filled card with a likely cause and the parts list to check
  4. The first visit is better prepared, fewer repeat trips
Takes off: back and forth questions that usually come before each repair

Where to start: One week: take one product line (for example Siegmund welding tables) and 20–30 past requests, set up AI to draft quotes from them, and compare with what managers actually sent. From KVERB: catalogs or price lists for that line and a few anonymised past requests with the offers.

What AI won't fix. AI will not set prices or discounts and will not replace the engineer on commissioning or repair. Final offer and price stay with the manager.
