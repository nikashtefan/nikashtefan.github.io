Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Spec and quote from a site description

  1. An engineer dictates the site: rooms, camera points, doors, network
  2. AI picks equipment from your brands and prices materials and labor
  3. A few minutes later a spec and quote arrive in your template
  4. The engineer checks it and sends it out
Takes off: building specs and quotes by hand for every site
idea 2

Reading tender documents

  1. Input: the tender specification
  2. AI lists the equipment and deadline requirements
  3. It flags items that need an equivalent product or a clarification
  4. The team decides whether to bid
Takes off: reading long tender specs by hand
idea 3

Answers to common client requests

  1. A client writes about cameras for a shop or office
  2. AI asks for details and puts together the request
  3. A manager gets a ready card
  4. The call goes straight to the point
Takes off: first calls spent figuring out what the client needs

Where to start: Start with CCTV for retail and offices for 2 weeks. From you: 10–15 past specs and quotes, price lists and your template.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace an engineer on a site with special security requirements. It prepares drafts. The team makes the decisions.
