Ocean Breeze: where AI could take manual work off

Beka Gogesashvili and Giorgi Chabradze, founders

79 projects in 2 years: AI can help build estimates and quotes for new MEP projects
I looked at the Ocean Breeze website and an article in Entrepreneur Georgia. In two years you have delivered 79 projects, including the Parliament of Georgia and European University, with a team of 53. With this kind of growth, it looks like estimates and quotes are still built by hand, and that is usually the first thing to slow down.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Estimate and quote from a project description

  1. An engineer dictates the project: area, use, which systems
  2. AI builds a spec based on your past projects and price lists
  3. A few minutes later a draft estimate and quote arrive
  4. The engineer checks it and sends it out
Takes off: building estimates and quotes by hand for every new project
idea 2

Merging supplier quotes

  1. Offers come in from Daikin, Midea, Wilo and others
  2. AI puts them into one table
  3. It shows the differences in price and lead time
  4. The founders or procurement decide
Takes off: comparing offers by hand
idea 3

Project status updates for clients

  1. Site managers send a short voice report
  2. AI builds a weekly report for the client
  3. The founders see all 6 projects in one place
  4. Fewer status calls
Takes off: collecting project reports by hand

Where to start: Start with one type of work for 2 weeks, for example HVAC for offices. From you: 10 past estimates and quotes, price lists and your template.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace an engineer on a complex project and will not take responsibility for calculations. It speeds up drafts. People still do the checking.
