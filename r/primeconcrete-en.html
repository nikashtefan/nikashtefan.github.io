Alexander Sokolowsky, CEO and founder

Port breakwaters with Jan De Nul and a dozen subcontractors. Subcontract paperwork can be ready the same day

It looks like comparing subcontractor offers and preparing contracts and reports is still done by hand.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Subcontractor contract package

  1. A subcontractor is chosen, their documents and price schedule arrive
  2. AI checks the documents and fills the contract and annexes in your template
  3. Legal reviews only the changes
  4. Contract goes for signature the same day
Takes off: waiting 1–2 days for each subcontract to be drafted
idea 2

Comparing supplier and subcontractor offers

  1. 3–5 offers arrive for rock, concrete, transport or testing
  2. AI puts them into one table: scope, schedule, exclusions
  3. Flags gaps against your bill of quantities
  4. Project manager decides in minutes
Takes off: manual side-by-side comparison of offers
idea 3

Weekly progress report

  1. Site teams send daily logs and photos
  2. AI builds a weekly report for the client and JV partner
  3. Planning engineer checks the numbers
  4. Report goes out on time
Takes off: hand-written weekly reports for the client and partners

Where to start: Take one work package on a current project for 2 weeks. We would need your contract template and 2–3 past subcontract packages.

What AI won't fix. AI won't judge whether a subcontractor can handle marine works or check quality on site. That stays with your engineers.
