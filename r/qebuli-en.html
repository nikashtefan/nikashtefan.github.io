Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Quote and spec from an installer's request

  1. An installer or manager dictates the project: area, floors, heating or air conditioning
  2. AI picks boilers, radiators and air conditioners from your stock and does the math
  3. It sends a quote and spec with stock availability
  4. A manager checks the discount and sends it out
Takes off: picking equipment and building quotes by hand for every project
idea 2

Choosing an air conditioner for a customer

  1. A customer writes the area and room type
  2. AI picks models by capacity and budget
  3. It prices standard installation on your terms
  4. A manager confirms the order
Takes off: routine product selection advice
idea 3

Stock answers across branches

  1. A customer asks if an item is available in Batumi or Zugdidi
  2. AI answers from current stock
  3. Harder questions go to a manager
  4. Fewer calls between branches
Takes off: calls and messages about availability

Where to start: Start with one category for 2 weeks, for example heating (boilers and radiators). From you: a product list export with prices, 20 past quotes and your template.

What AI won't fix. AI will not replace a manager in work with large construction companies and will not give discounts on its own. It takes over routine product selection and paperwork.
