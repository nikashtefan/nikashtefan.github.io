Levan Mdinaradze, Founder

I read the Georgia Today piece about Rodeo, your new arm for technical production and equipment rental. Redbow works with 150+ corporate clients in Tbilisi and Dubai. It looks like every event needs its own budget, quotes from venues and suppliers, and contracts with freelance crews, and that is mostly done by hand.

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Contracts with freelancers and suppliers in minutes

  1. A freelancer or supplier sends their details and agreed fee
  2. AI fills your contract template and the scope for this event
  3. The manager checks it
  4. The contract is signed before the event, not after
Takes off: retyping details into contracts for every crew member
idea 2

Event quote from a brief

  1. The client sends a brief: date, guests, format
  2. AI builds a draft quote from your price list and past events, Rodeo gear included
  3. The manager adjusts and adds the creative part
  4. The client gets a quote the same day
Takes off: building every budget from scratch in a spreadsheet
idea 3

Gear and crew plan for each event

  1. The confirmed quote lists equipment and roles
  2. AI makes a load-in list and crew schedule
  3. Rodeo checks availability
  4. Everyone gets their part of the plan
Takes off: manual lists and back-and-forth in chats before each event

Where to start: Start with freelancer and supplier contracts for the next 2–3 events. I would need your contract template and a few past contracts.

What AI won't fix. AI will not come up with the creative concept or run the event on the day. It handles the paperwork around it.
