Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

AI writes the insights and recommendations in the monthly report

  1. Monthly numbers from Google Ads, Meta, TikTok and Telegram Ads and analytics land in one table
  2. AI compares them with last month and the plan, and finds what went up, what dropped and why
  3. AI drafts the "insights and next steps" section in the client's language; the account manager checks and edits it
  4. The client gets a report with clear conclusions, not just tables
Takes off: hours that account managers and specialists spend writing insights for every monthly report
idea 2

Daily ad account check for budget leaks

  1. Every day AI looks at campaigns in Google Ads, Meta and TikTok Ads
  2. It looks for leaks: spend with no conversions, rising cost per lead, click campaigns where reach or leads are needed
  3. It sends the specialist a short list in Telegram: where money is lost and what to fix
  4. The specialist decides and fixes it, and the findings go into the monthly report
Takes off: checking ad accounts by hand and the risk of spotting a leak only at month end
idea 3

Answering client questions about the report

  1. A client writes in chat: "why did cost per lead go up in October?"
  2. AI looks at ad account and report data and drafts an answer with numbers
  3. The account manager checks and sends it
  4. Recurring questions get answered in the next report in advance
Takes off: digging through ad accounts for numbers for every client question

Where to start: Start with report insights for 3–5 clients for 2 weeks. From you: 2–3 past reports that clients liked, guest access to their ad accounts or data exports, and your report template.

What AI won't fix. AI does not know your agreements with the client or what was said on a call. The account manager keeps the strategy and the decision on what to tell the client. AI only prepares a draft.
