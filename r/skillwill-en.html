Skillwill College: where AI could take manual work off

Giorgi Mariamidze, founder and CEO

From 2 colleges to 12: an AI assistant can handle contracts with new lecturers
I looked at the news about the colleges opening in Tbilisi and Batumi, the plan for 12 colleges and the launch in Kutaisi. Every new city needs its own lecturers and mentors. It looks like for each of them someone collects documents and prepares a contract by hand, and with 10 new colleges this work will grow many times over.

What I looked at

  • On 25.11.2025, 1 350 students started studying at the colleges in Tbilisi and Batumi · bag.ge, 28.11.2025
  • Plan: 12 colleges across the country, 10 of them new, including Kutaisi, Rustavi, Zugdidi, Telavi, Gori; up to 10 000 students a year · bag.ge, 28.11.2025

Where AI could take manual work off

idea 1

Lecturer contract without collecting documents by hand

  1. A new lecturer sends photos of documents and bank details via Telegram or a link
  2. AI checks the details, finds errors and empty fields, and asks the lecturer to fix them right away
  3. AI fills in the contract from your template: college, program, workload, dates
  4. A college staff member gets a ready contract to check and sign
Takes off: back-and-forth with lecturers for their details and filling in contracts by hand at every new college
idea 2

Answering applicants in new cities

  1. An applicant writes on Facebook or the website: city, what they want to study
  2. AI explains the programs at the nearest college and the state funding terms
  3. AI signs them up for pre-registration and reminds them of deadlines
  4. The admissions team sees ready applications
Takes off: the same questions about programs, deadlines and funding in every city
idea 3

Help desk for the team at a new college

  1. Internal rules, admission rules and answers to common questions go into one base
  2. A staff member at a new college asks the assistant
  3. AI answers from the base with a link to the right document
  4. Unanswered questions go to the head office and are added to the base
Takes off: calls to the head office from staff at new colleges

Where to start: Start with lecturer contracts at one new college for 2 weeks. From you: the contract template, the list of details you need, and 3–5 anonymized examples.

What AI won't fix. AI will not find lecturers for you and will not decide who is stronger. A person agrees contract terms and handles disputed cases.
